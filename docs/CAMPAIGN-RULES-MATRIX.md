# Campaign Rules Classification

Source reviewed: _The Hunt in Estalia Campaign_, draft dated October 2026, 26 pages.

This document translates the supplied campaign draft into software responsibilities without
copying game-specific prose into the generic application. It is analysis, not a replacement
for the campaign rules.

## Classification key

- **Enforce:** reject or transform invalid commands on the authoritative server.
- **Calculate:** derive state, resources, points, or assignments.
- **Secret:** apply field/record-level authorization and prevent client over-fetching.
- **Audit:** preserve actor, reason, time, before/after, and revision.
- **Configure:** campaign staff provide structured data/rules.
- **Display:** show reference text; do not simulate tabletop behavior.
- **TBD:** incomplete or ambiguous; agents must not invent it.

## Application rule matrix

| Rule area | Classification | Initial software responsibility |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Campaign length and round schedule | Configure, Enforce, Audit | Configure rounds/deadlines and per-phase "End phase early if able to resolve" (default on); after launch allow round-count increase and lengthening remaining windows, not shortening them. |
| Two action windows and battle phase | Enforce | Maintain explicit timed states; Action 1 battle can force later Battle action. When early-close is off, an idle battle phase stays open until its deadline so a GM can inject a ringer fight; a battle phase with finalized engagements and committed retreats still ends. |
| Draft, commit, uncommit, early close | Enforce, Audit | A confirmed map action or force-panel checkmark saves a draft. Commit is allowed only after every required force that is not in battle and not waiting to teleport has a saved draft. A force locked into a two-phase teleport is ready without a player draft and is auto-committed when it is the player's only remaining order. The latest draft commits at the deadline or on commit. Uncommit back to draft only while the action window is open. Close atomically when early-close is on, including when nobody owes because remaining forces are in battle or waiting to teleport. Unused battle time flows into the next window. |
| Campaign play log | Audit, Display | Show a log on the campaign page (upcoming through completed) with timestamps and originators; load that log separately from campaign metadata so chat does not wait on the rest of the page; record campaign start, named round/phase openings, campaign close, a final-score and remaining revealed item-objective snapshot when the campaign ends (a later manager score or item adjustment appends an updated snapshot), schedule extensions, revealed actions including Hold, interrupted actions cancelled with a reason, invalid or conflicting submitted actions that became Hold, battles, manager battle-result overrides, retreats, automatic force rejoins, force status changes with their source, and revealed rival-objective victories; group action-phase resolution facts by owning player then battle locks, and battle-phase results before retreats; bold campaign start, round/phase openings, campaign end, and campaign close; accept member chat to public, direct, faction, or ally-group channels with `@` tags of current members only; let composers type and autocomplete Everyone or a member username; link chat originators and mentions to public profiles; omit private chats from unauthorized payloads, including campaign managers; never log unrevealed orders or site-wide chat; let a manager or administrator download public chat and/or game-log facts as one text or CSV file at any lifecycle stage, omitting private chat. |
| Site chat | Display, Notification | Show a public site-wide chat on All Campaigns, separate from campaign logs; allow `@` tags of any account; reject prohibited language; let users block one another so player messages hide both ways; let administrators announce to everyone or one person with notifications, showing those announcements in bold; flag and filter languages without translating. |
| Participants and public profiles | Display, Secret | List attached members with faction and Manager/Player/Admin roles; open public profiles from member names; list only campaigns the viewer may already view. |
| Notifications and home board | Notification, Display | Notify in-app and/or by email of mentions, private chats, campaign start, campaign end, new phases, actions that still need the user, site-chat mentions, administrator site-chat announcements, and manager delinquency kick recommendations from the third offence; show those items on Home five per page newest first with timestamps, dismiss and dismiss all, or "No new notifications." when none remain; email bodies omit hidden orders, private chat text, and site-chat bodies. |
| Site news | Display | Show administrator-authored markdown news on Home, two articles per page. |
| Missing order | Enforce | Create Hold for every missing required force/action slot. A force with no draft at all is a delinquency offence; an uncommitted draft is not. |
| Move | Enforce, Calculate | Validate controller, origin, destination, adjacency, spawn restrictions (pass through own spawn only; never land on a spawn), force movement speed (default 1; Bretonnia preset 2; item bonuses; CalledByTheRelic +1 while a revealed item exists and no force of that faction holds an item), and a named via for every hop, including a unique legal route when speed is 2 or more. An enemy on the path stops the force there; allies do not interrupt. Only the landing territory is claimed. Invalid Move becomes Hold. |
| Hold | Enforce, Calculate | Preserve location and trigger configured rest/status effects. |
| Build | Enforce, Calculate | Validate structure slot and a buildable type; create owner/controller state. |
| Pillage | Enforce, Calculate | Owner may pillage own structures; allies cannot unless `OnlyBloodSatisfies`. Validate pillageable flags; progress operational to pillaged and award pillage temporary supply. |
| Destroy | Enforce, Calculate | Remove a destructible structure (pillaged, or operational when `OnlyBloodSatisfies`). Award pillage supply, doubled for `OnlyBloodSatisfies` destroying an unpillaged structure. |
| Repair | Enforce, Calculate | Current territory owner or a current ally of that owner may restore a pillaged structure. |
| Split/rejoin forces | Enforce, Calculate, Audit | Enforce force limit and target eligibility; independently track split forces; automatically rejoin co-located same-player forces into one action and log the rejoin. Split uses the same named hop path as Move. |
| Backstab | Enforce, Calculate, Audit | Only when sharing a territory with an allied force or occupying that ally's empty land. Breaks the alliance for the acting player only. Optional subfactions betray the whole victim faction; required or `DividedWeStand` subfactions betray that subfaction only. Empty former-ally land: claim and auto-pillage (never auto-destroy). Shared land: create a battle with no auto-pillage. Traitor gains no supply or allied-relic points from the betrayed faction. |
| Retreat | Enforce, Calculate, Audit | Choose a destination then commit or uncommit from the map toolbar like Action-phase orders while the battle window remains open. Validate eligible destinations (owned, allied, or Neutral, each reachable at movement speed without meeting enemies or crossing a battle; allied occupation of the destination is allowed; Art of War any non-enemy-spawn), send every enemy faction that would land on the same hex to spawn, allow pass-through of another enemy's landing hex, auto-commit when only one legal destination remains (including spawn), submit an uncommitted draft at the deadline, and assign spawn when no order is given or no eligible destination remains. A finalized battle still waiting on a committed retreat is shown as Awaiting Retreat Order. |
| Automatic Battle | Calculate | Create engagement when enemy forces remain together after precedence resolution. |
| Surrender | Enforce, Calculate, Audit | Allowed while engaged in an action or battle window. Click the force on the map, choose a destination, and commit from the map toolbar. Committed surrender may be uncommitted while the current window remains open; uncommitting restores the engagement so the opponent does not keep max BP. A draft still executes at the deadline. 1v1 clicks the remaining player through at max differential battle points and 0 for the surrenderer, with no extra BP and no result agreement. Voluntary surrender is not a missed-result delinquency for either side. Remaining side wins if everyone else runs; mutual run is a no-contest. |
| Multiple combatants | Calculate | Allied extras add 25 percent round army points per extra player, split evenly, round up to 10. More than two opposing sides: two strongest play first, then strongest-to-weakest in the same phase; unplayed forces stay for the next battle phase. A correction joiner waits, then plays remaining forces in that sequence. |
| Allied co-location | Calculate | Allies cannot claim allied land without backstab. Two allies on Neutral: strongest claims. Allied occupants defend without taking the flag. Required-subfaction claims plant that subfaction's flag or logo instead of the parent faction's. |
| Map image and territories | Configure | Upload raster, draw/import polygons, assign IDs, terrain, ownership, spawn, structures, structure condition, and adjacency. A pair of territories has at most one connection. |
| Spawn behavior | Enforce, Calculate | Prohibit enemy entry/battle/build, provide base supply, combine same-player split forces, and never land a Move or teleport on a spawn. Forces may pass through their own spawn on a multi-territory Move but cannot land on any spawn except a forced retreat to their spawn. |
| Terrain types | Configure, Display | Select mission/layout candidates and show tabletop rules. Standard terrain is a replaceable preset copied from the current catalog. Each type has a Water feature flag for special-rule interaction and player reminders; it does not change movement by itself. Terrain and structure mission lists attach catalog or one-off missions. |
| Mission assignment | Configure, Calculate, Display | Structure missions if any, otherwise terrain. Prefer attacker/defender missions for Hold/Retreat vs Move/Split, owned-structure or allied defense, or backstab; otherwise only when no normal mission remains. Show the chosen mission (and file/URL) on the campaign battle panel. Army-point advantages clamp at 500; supply advantages clamp at 1. |
| Tabletop terrain features | Display | Present woods, water, river, bridge, cliff, castle, and similar rules with the mission; do not simulate tabletop movement. |
| Supply network | Configure, Calculate, Display | Graph traversal from each force through owned or allied (same group, not backstabbed by that player) territories it can reach. Terrain and operational owned or allied structures grant configured supply points (new rows default 1; omitted or invalid values are 0) to every force connected to that chain. Structure campaign points stay with the territory owner. Summary lists the viewer's faction, then each force with its chain supply dropdown, then the shared spendable (temporary) pool. Participants and battles still show the maximum one force can spend, with a hover tooltip and collapsed per-source breakdown (territories, structures, allied holdings, bonuses, temporary pool, split penalty), plus the army-point cap for that game. |
| Temporary supply | Configure, Calculate, Audit | Award configured pillage/destroy points (new rows default 1; omitted or invalid values are 0) to the earning player. That player may spend the pool on any of their forces; each point applies to exactly one force. Battle reports spend supply-costing units from force allowance first, then this pool. |
| Split-force supply | Configure, Calculate | Each split force claims the map supply of the chain it can reach minus the split penalty (Hunt default raw 1, or a configured 0–100 percent; a configured 0 applies no penalty), plus the full round free-supply bonus. Temporary supply remains a shared player pool. |
| Army escalation | Configure, Calculate | Configure one row per round for max army points (10–100000), free supply, and free characters. Generic default is 1000/1/1; Hunt in Estalia uses its eight-round table. |
| Battle result reporting | Configure, Enforce, Audit | Players report both sides' tabletop outcome. Each player must enter their own army points and supply-costing units; army-list text is optional. Agree, dispute, staff confirm, or deadline-commit. One timely submission is authoritative. Neither submission is a no-contest forced retreat (skip Shaken, no spoils). True BP ties force both to retreat without a loss. Campaign managers configure reusable standard result questions and attach them to missions, with optional per-mission point overrides; unique per-mission questions remain. |
| Army-list submission | Configure, Display | Optional pasted list text is stored on the report and shown to the participant, opponent, and staff. Warhammer: The Old World lists from New Recruit or Old World Builder may auto-fill category supply amounts; Other and unrecognized text leave amounts to the player. Full army-list legality is outside scope. |
| Campaign presets | Configure | Built-in Hunt in Estalia catalog plus administrator-saved named presets (from Edit campaign or Edit map) that include map image, overlay graph, and uploaded logos/flags, remapped by catalog name on apply. Duplicate names after trimming whitespace overwrite. Administrators may download/upload a portable preset ZIP between hosts, including those catalog files. Re-upload of the same collapsed name overwrites the named preset and reuses identical map/overlay/catalog files. Hunt Daemons of Chaos subfactions use unique color flags. |
| Army composition | Calculate, Display | Calculate campaign-level supply allowance/cost; full army-list legality is outside initial scope. |
| Campaign points | Configure, Calculate, Display | Shown while a campaign is in progress or completed, not while upcoming. Show a running per-player table of that player's territory and structure holdings (including configured points per territory), cumulative battle campaign points, awarded ranking and named public objectives, allied relic control, and visible held item-objective points. Shared faction totals are not copied onto every co-faction player. Omit hidden item sources from unauthorized totals. Each enabled public objective that is not an item objective shows a top five (allied relic control is omitted). At most five rows; overflowing ties are summarized as how many players share that value. 0-point objectives are ignored. |
| Longest connected territory chain | Calculate | Calculate from player-owned territory graph; allied territories excluded. Friendly first-place ties each receive the configured campaign points. |
| Structure-control objective | Calculate | Rank by currently owned non-destroyed structure campaign points. Friendly first-place ties each receive the configured campaign points. |
| Points per territory | Calculate, Display | When configured above 0, each currently owned territory awards that many public-objective campaign points. Show a top five by current territory count. |
| Allied relic control | Calculate | When configured above 0, each revealed relic held by another player of the same faction or a current ally awards that many public-objective campaign points. A traitor does not count relics held by the faction or scoped subfaction they betrayed, and that faction does not count the traitor's relics. |
| Public objectives | Configure, Calculate, Display | Track progress and show criteria/results. |
| Private objectives | Configure, Secret, Calculate, Audit | Catalog at setup; at launch assign one secret objective from each player, faction, and ally-group pool. Traitor-kind entries are not seeded. Skip empty pools. Unique draws first, then reshuffled duplicates so every holder in a non-empty pool receives an assignment, except a player never holds the same catalog type twice (player-held and Traitor-held share that uniqueness). Required subfactions each receive a separate faction-pool assignment and score only that subfaction's holdings. Player-held automatic progress counts only that player's credited holdings and actions. Each catalog entry may exclude factions and ally groups; excluded holders cannot receive it, it must remain receivable by at least one faction, and unique assignment onto factions must remain possible until the catalog is larger than the faction count (most restrictive first). Manual claims need manager approval. Automatic criteria score from map facts and become public when completed. Unclaimed manual objectives do not score when the campaign ends. The viewer's own assignments, including Traitor-held copies and the active rival, are listed in Private objectives and Summary; other holders' claimed or revealed objectives appear in a collapsed subpanel. Unclaimed text stays secret until reveal or campaign completion. |
| Rival objectives | Configure, Secret, Calculate, Audit | Optional campaign flag, on by default, with configurable campaign points (default 5). When enabled, each occupying player receives a secret rival who is a specific enemy, not an ally. Unique rivals first; duplicates only when uniqueness is impossible. Victory is a finalized battle win or rival surrender; draws, delinquency, no-contest, and ringer battles do not count. The holder sees the rival's name, faction, and subfaction. Revealed points go in the Private Objectives standings column. Replenish at the start of a later non-final action round for players who already had a rival and now have none, choosing the closest unused enemy and skipping current battle opponents. Omit entirely when the flag is off. |
| Traitor private objective | Calculate, Secret | After a successful Backstab, grant one extra Traitor-pool objective per distinct betrayed relationship. Recycle the pool if it runs out; a player never holds or scores the same catalog type twice. |
| Hidden item-objective placement | Secret, Configure | Random or manager-placed; skip spawn unless allowed; never include unrevealed location in player responses. |
| Item-objective discovery/transfer | Calculate, Audit | Reveal when found or staff-revealed in debug. A force may hold more than one item. Optional drop of unopened items at the start of Move; pickup by a lone force not in battle; two or more occupying forces reveal a hidden or found unclaimed item without a possessor; a battle winner takes every held and unclaimed item in the battle. Log copy uses the player name for pickup and drop. |
| Relic choices/effects | Configure, Secret, Calculate, Audit | Configure flavor text, named choices, and parameterized holder effects in setup (movement, supply, Teleport Randomly over two action phases with a secret Neutral or allied destination, Teleport to Specific Territory with a 3-phase Hold-accelerated recharge, statuses including optional success/failure statuses, nullify adjacent items, army points, alliance overrides, or custom battle reminders). Resolving a choice applies one result or a random result from that choice's group: new flavor text, optional new state label, optional secret private objective, and optional destroy-and-replace. Destroyed items award no points and leave the map. |
| Force statuses | Calculate, Display, Audit | Track one named status per force (no named status is Normal and is not shown as a status chip). Unique priorities 0-999 (0 highest) decide which status remains when more than one would apply. A status may cancel another to no status. Enable and clear each list one or more OR conditions (a trigger, 1-10 consecutive matches, an optional any/type/tag location, a chosen occupying status, or a chosen standard battle-result question; at least one of each after save collapse). Battle triggers count consecutive battles; occupying and cut-off triggers count consecutive action phases; reunion triggers fire when a claim restores a missing spawn or structure route. The same trigger may repeat when the location, occupying status, or question differs. Named Diseased uses Water-tag catalog conditions plus contagion, rejoin, plague-bearing wins, and immunity; Hold at a Capital City, City, Supply Depot, or Town clears it to no status. Missions and item results may list ordered status-change conditions. Each status may list immune factions and named subfactions, plus an optional chit or token image. Hunt in Estalia fills those immunities on the standard catalog. Empty lists keep older special-rule refusals (`Undead`, `CalledByTheRelic`, `BringersOfThePlague`, `ToughGuts`). The campaign page shows a collapsed reference of effects, conditions, cancel-out, current forces, and immune factions. Managers and administrators may assign any catalog status. Effect text is display-only. Status changes are logged with source. |
| Faction choice | Configure, Enforce | Players pick a faction (and required subfaction) before they can play; they may change it until the campaign starts, then it is locked. Managers and administrators may assign another player's faction after launch for fixes and testing. |
| Faction alignments | Configure | Model alliance groups and unaligned factions per campaign. Renaming a group keeps assigned factions in that group. |
| Faction map rules | Configure, Enforce/Calculate | Structured modifiers may affect movement, supply, spawn, relic sensing, status, pillage, or retreat. |
| Faction tabletop rules | Configure, Display | Reusable special-rule catalog (name, description, optional effect key) assigned to factions, subfactions, and item objectives. Hunt in Estalia copies named faction-sheet wording. User-created rules are display-only and do not execute code. |
| Rule/version changes | Configure, Audit | Record source/version/effective round; activate approved changes at a round boundary. |
| GM order inspection | Secret, Audit | Permit inspection only inside a logged debug session; unrevealed orders stay out of the public log. Notify affected players when debug ends. |
| GM corrections | Enforce, Audit | Enter debug (logged), append staff corrections without overwriting originals, re-resolve the last window only while the following phase is open (or post-campaign grace), uncommit or nullify only directly affected current-phase orders, then exit debug (logged, notifying players). |
| Multiple GMs | Enforce, Audit | Support concurrent campaign memberships and optimistic concurrency. |
| Neutral intervention | Configure, Audit | Ephemeral GM ringer battle against an idle player force in an open battle phase; ringer leaves no map trace. Drought occupation is out of scope. |
| Delinquency | Enforce, Audit, Notification | Per-force campaign-lifetime count of missing drafts, missing required retreats, and neither-side missed results. Notify managers from the third offence as a possible kick; never auto-remove. Participants on in-progress and completed campaigns show the player's count and an expandable offence list (round, phase, window end, territory). |

## Faction-rule implementation categories

The supplied faction rules demonstrate why modifiers need explicit categories rather than
arbitrary scripts.

### Map-enforced or calculated

- Extended movement with intermediate-territory interception.
- Extra structure supply.
- Pillage strength and allowed targets.
- Status immunity/transfer.
- Retreat destination exceptions.
- Hidden-relic proximity hint and relic-directed movement.
- Terrain-as-structure supply/defense.
- Alternative supply generation.
- Alternative/random spawn behavior.
- Forced movement toward a revealed relic.
- Extra temporary supply from pillage.
- Extra Black Powder spend declared on the battle result.
- Magical Supply leftover rerolls declared on the battle result (capped at unused composition supply).
- Per-battle army lists shown to the opponent (parse helper does not block result agreement).

### Display or battle metadata

- Ambush modifiers.
- One-use battle abilities.
- Terrain-specific tabletop modifiers.
- Army unit eligibility and mercenary effects.

The first group may use tested, typed policy implementations. The second group is reference
content or structured mission metadata. Do not execute administrator-provided code.

## Unfinished or ambiguous draft areas

These remain in `DECISIONS-NEEDED.md` and must not be silently resolved by an agent:

- Castle siege mechanics (gates, walls, battering rams, scaling, and inside-the-walls) are out of
  application scope. Castle remains a structure type; tabletop castle features stay display-only.
- Armies-of-infamy content is out of application scope. Those lists are ordinary subfaction
  configuration and may be added later as preset or catalog data.
- Relic choice options, result groups, destroy-and-replace, and granted secret objectives are configured in setup. Named Hunt special-rule effect keys are enforced or calculated as documented in DOMAIN.md; remaining tabletop dice effects stay display-only.
- Exact proprietary battle-point conversion charts are out of scope; managers enter already-converted
  scores or raw victory points and configure multiplier, clamp, and negative-loser behavior.

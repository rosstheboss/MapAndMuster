Privacy

Map & Muster stores the account details you submit so you can sign in and play campaigns: email, username, name, location, time zone, optional profile picture, and the campaigns, maps, chat, and orders you create.

If you choose Google, Discord, or Facebook sign-in, that provider sends us the name, email, and optional profile picture you already granted to that provider. We do not receive your provider password.

Session cookies keep you signed in. We send transactional email for address confirmation, password reset, and the notifications you enable. We do not sell account data.

This page exists so OAuth consoles can link a public privacy URL for Map & Muster.

Cookies

This notice describes cookies and similar storage used by Map & Muster. It is written for visitors in the United Kingdom, the European Union, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. You can accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or change that choice later from this page’s banner controls in the site footer.

Essential cookies

These are required to operate the site and do not need prior consent under UK PECR, EU ePrivacy, or equivalent rules. They authenticate a signed-in or guest session (mapandmuster.auth), complete external sign-in (campaign.external), and remember your cookie choice (cookie_consent). Guest preview also uses the essential session cookie and ends after 24 hours, when you log out, or when that cookie is removed.

Preference cookies

These remember display choices: light or dark theme (theme), public chat language filters (siteChat), and per-campaign map and panel layout (cv-*). The map editor may also store overlay color mode in local storage. Preference storage is off until you accept it. Rejecting non-essential cookies still lets you use the site, including guest preview and sign-in.

Your choices

Accept all turns preference storage on. Reject non-essential keeps only essential cookies and deletes stored preferences. Cookie settings lets you change that later. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. See Privacy for account data.
